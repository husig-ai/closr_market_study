EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Should Husig Build Closr?

YES — STRONG GO

Based on analysis of 17 survey responses from real estate professionals, Closr demonstrates clear market validation with strong demand signals across all key metrics.

🎯 Market Opportunity

  • 71% beta interest — 10 respondents said "Yes, definitely" to early access, with 5 more as "Maybe"
  • 59% want automation — "Yes, absolutely" to auto follow-ups and appointment booking
  • 47% willing to pay $50+/month — Sweet spot pricing tier at $50-99/mo
  • 35% have no CRM — 6 respondents are greenfield opportunities with no incumbent to displace

🔥 Pain Points to Solve

  • #1: Too many unqualified leads (9 mentions, 53%) — AI lead qualification is the top priority
  • #2: Leads go cold (7 mentions, 41%) — Automated follow-up sequences are critical
  • #3: Slow follow-up time (7 mentions, 41%) — Speed-to-lead automation needed
  • #4: Manual data entry (5 mentions, 29%) — CRM auto-population from conversations

⭐ Must-Have Features

  • Smart follow-up reminders — 14 requests (82%)
  • Automatic lead qualification — 12 requests (71%)
  • Personalized property recommendations — 12 requests (71%)
  • Email/SMS automation — 10 requests (59%)
  • Market insights & price prediction — 9 requests (53%)

👥 Target Customer Profile

  • Experience: 1-7 years (76% of respondents)
  • Volume: 6-30 transactions/year (mid-tier producers)
  • Structure: Independent agents (59%) and small teams
  • Focus: Primarily residential (65%)
  • Current state: Using manual notes or basic CRM, spending 2-5 hours/week on lead tasks

📋 Recommended MVP Scope

P1 AI Lead Scoring & Qualification
P1 Automated Follow-up Sequences (SMS/WhatsApp)
P1 Message Review Mode (human-in-loop option)
P2 Property Matching Recommendations
P2 Calendar/Appointment Booking

⚠️ Key Risks & Mitigations

Small sample size (n=17)
→ Run larger survey or validate with paid ads landing page test
6 respondents prefer manual communication
→ Include message approval workflow as a core feature
Budget sensitivity (8 want to pay <$50)
→ Consider freemium tier or usage-based pricing
Bottom Line: The data shows a clear market need with strong willingness to adopt and pay. Recommend proceeding with MVP development focused on lead qualification and automated follow-ups, with 10 confirmed beta testers ready to provide feedback.
